## DESIGN DIRECTION

**Tone:** Bold, confident, human. Not corporate and cold.
**Aesthetic:** Dark background, large white typography,
full-screen imagery, clean minimal layout
**Reference:** reyesholdings.com — study this site before
choosing a template or making any design decisions
**Imagery:** Real photos taken at SSB facility, leveled
up using Adobe Firefly or Midjourney. No stock photos.
**Typography:** Large, bold, modern. Headlines should stop
the eye.
**Color:** Dark base (near black or deep navy), white
typography, one accent color pulled from the CrazyCups
brand palette
**CTAs:** Two styles — primary (filled button) and
secondary (outline button)

### AI & INNOVATION PAGE

Page Headline: AI Is Not a Feature. It's How We Operate.

Page Subheadline: Since our founding we have believed
that technology is the great equalizer — allowing a lean,
focused team to outperform much larger competitors. Today
AI is embedded across every function of our business.

Six capability blocks:

1. Demand Forecasting
We use AI to predict what customers will buy, when they
will buy it, and in what quantities — reducing waste and
keeping our brands available when demand spikes.

2. Inventory Management
AI-assisted inventory systems that monitor stock levels,
flag risk, and trigger replenishment automatically — so
we never miss a sale due to an out-of-stock.

3. Content Creation
From product descriptions to email campaigns to social
content — AI generates, tests, and optimizes our
messaging at a scale impossible with a traditional
marketing team.

4. Customer Acquisition
AI-powered advertising and audience targeting that finds
our next customer before our competitors do — across
Amazon, Meta, Google, and beyond.

5. Competitive Analysis
We monitor competitor pricing, reviews, and positioning
in real time using AI — allowing us to respond to market
shifts faster than any legacy player.

6. Product Development
AI-assisted trend analysis and consumer insight tools
that help us identify the next winning product before the
category catches up.

Closing statement:
This is not the future. This is how we operate today.
